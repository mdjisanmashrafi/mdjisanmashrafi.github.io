Current research

Earth Copilot: A Continual Multimodal World Model for Dynamic Earth Observation and Long-Term Environmental Intelligence

MPhil research, UTM. Ongoing proposal, not a completed system.

Most multimodal Earth observation models stay static after pretraining and cannot adapt to changing environmental conditions. Earth Copilot proposes a persistent, dynamic latent representation of the Earth that learns continually from streaming observations.

Objectives

  • Represent: integrate optical, SAR and elevation data in one latent state.
  • Adapt: learn continually while mitigating catastrophic forgetting.
  • Reason: support long-term environmental reasoning, anomaly detection and scenario-based decision-making.

Data modalities

  • Optical imagery
  • SAR
  • DEM
  • Ancillary geospatial data

Approach

  • Transformer-based fusion
  • Adaptive multimodal fusion
  • Selective replay
  • Regularization
  • Concept drift detection

Outputs

  • Anomaly detection
  • Long-term trend tracking
  • Environmental reasoning
  • What-if scenarios
Review manuscript: Continual Multimodal World Models for Earth Observation: A Review of Foundations, Challenges, and Environmental Intelligence.
About this review

Traces the evolution from static remote sensing classifiers to adaptive, physics-aware environmental intelligence architectures capable of long-term planetary-scale simulation and autonomous reasoning.
