About

I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, where I am pursuing an MPhil in Artificial Intelligence with Prof. Shreeshivadasan Chelliapan and Dr. Siti Sophiayati Binti Yuhaniz.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University (2021–2025). My undergraduate thesis proposed ReCAN, a lightweight attention network for medical image classification, later published in BMC Medical Imaging. That work, together with applied research on climate, hydrology and agriculture, moved my focus from deep learning and computer vision toward models that keep learning from evolving, multimodal data.

My current work covers efficient deep learning, continual learning, multimodal learning and foundation models. I collaborate with researchers at York University (Lassonde School of Engineering) and King Abdulaziz University (Faculty of Environmental Sciences), and I aim to build adaptive AI systems that reason reliably over changing data.

Current research

Earth Copilot: A Continual Multimodal World Model for Dynamic Earth Observation and Long-Term Environmental Intelligence

MPhil research · UTM · ongoing proposal, not a completed system

Motivation. Multimodal foundation models have improved Earth observation, but most stay static after pretraining and cannot adapt as environmental conditions change.

Objective. Learn a persistent, dynamic latent representation of the Earth from streaming observations, supporting long-term environmental reasoning, anomaly detection and scenario-based decision-making.

  • Optical imagery
  • Synthetic Aperture Radar
  • Digital Elevation Models
  • Continual learning
  • Adaptive multimodal fusion
  • Concept drift detection
  • Anomaly detection
  • Scenario-based decision-making

Review manuscript

Continual Multimodal World Models for Earth Observation: A Review of Foundations, Challenges, and Environmental Intelligence. Under peer review · International Journal of Computer Vision

A review tracing the evolution from static remote sensing classifiers to adaptive, physics-aware environmental intelligence architectures capable of long-term planetary-scale simulation and autonomous reasoning.
