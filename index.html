MPhil Research
Earth Copilot: A Continual Multimodal World Model
Ongoing Research — Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)
Developing a continual multimodal world model designed for dynamic Earth observation and long-term environmental intelligence. The system integrates optical imagery, SAR, DEM, and geospatial data through transformer-based fusion to detect anomalies, track long-term trends, and provide "what-if" scenario analysis.
Abstract: Recent advances in multimodal foundation models have significantly improved Earth observation; however, most existing models remain static after pretraining and cannot continuously adapt to evolving environmental conditions. This research proposes Earth Copilot, a continual multimodal world model that learns a dynamic latent representation of the Earth by integrating optical imagery, synthetic aperture radar (SAR), digital elevation models, and ancillary geospatial data. The proposed framework combines transformer-based multimodal fusion with continual learning techniques, including selective replay, regularization, adaptive fusion, and concept drift detection, to enable lifelong learning from streaming satellite observations while mitigating catastrophic forgetting. Unlike conventional Earth observation models that perform isolated prediction tasks, Earth Copilot maintains a persistent environmental representation to support long-term reasoning, anomaly detection, and scenario-based decision-making.
